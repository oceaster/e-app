Choosing your e-server is now a step of its own. After you sign in, the client shows the e-servers saved to your Easter Company account instead of jumping straight back to the last one you used: pick one and that server asks for its PIN, remove one you no longer use from the list, or add a new one by its HTTPS address (the client checks that an e-server answers there before saving it). The list belongs to your account, so it follows you to another machine; sign in there and your servers are already listed.

The client also asks twice as rarely about a wrong thing: choosing the server you are already connected to raises that window instead of asking a question with one answer.

e-server desktop client for Windows 10+ (x64), Ubuntu, Arch Linux and Linux AppImage (x86_64). Windows installers are unsigned; verify the source repository and SHA256SUMS before running. Linux AppImage may require FUSE 2.
